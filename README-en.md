I'm **Nzongo Pedro**, also known as **Mr. PHP**, an Angolan Software and Telecommunications Engineer focused on building web systems, APIs, infrastructure and connected solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of **software engineering, telecommunications, DevOps, cloud infrastructure, IoT and technical education**.

## Selected Projects

### ColeGius

Multi-tenant school management platform designed to centralize academic and administrative operations.

**Focus:** Laravel, databases, authentication, multi-tenancy, academic management and web architecture.

### Corporate E-Learning & Compliance Platform

Corporate training platform focused on Compliance, Risk Management, AML/CFT, Ethics, Governance, Audit, Internal Control, ESG and Data Protection.

The platform is designed around corporate requirements such as mandatory training, assessments, certificates, evidence of completion and auditability.

**Focus:** Laravel, APIs, databases, authentication, multimedia content, reporting and enterprise architecture.

### EduBridge

Educational support platform connecting students who require financial assistance with supporters willing to contribute toward educational objectives.

**Architecture:** student, supporter and administrator environments with contribution tracking and campaign management.

### KwanzaGuard

Concept focused on building technology for financial protection, fraud awareness and digital trust in the Angolan context.

**Focus:** software engineering, security concepts, financial technology and local digital ecosystems.

### Smart Home IoT

IoT prototype integrating ESP32, sensors, actuators and a Laravel backend.

The system includes environmental monitoring, presence detection, lighting automation, ventilation control and event communication between embedded hardware and web services.

**Technologies:** ESP32, Arduino, sensors, I2C, UART, Laravel, REST API and web interfaces.
